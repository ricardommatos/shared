CT-16 · sampling instrumentCode and Theory
Introducing

CT-16.

A sampling instrument you can hold in one hand and play with both.

01 · Form

Thin as a sketchbook.
Dense as a studio.

One machined slab. Every control sits flat on the surface, nothing hidden under a menu.

02 · Screen

A screen that shows the sound.

Two waveform lanes, the step pattern and the tempo, lit behind glass.

03 · Keys

Sixteen keys.
Every one a drum.

Kick to crash, laid out in a grid your fingers learn in minutes.

04 · Control

Four knobs.
One orange button.

Tempo, filter, space and pitch under your fingertips. Press play and it runs.

05 · Inside

Built in layers.

Keys, encoders, screen and body, each part its own piece.
